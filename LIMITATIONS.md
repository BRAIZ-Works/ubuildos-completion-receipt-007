# Limitations and non-claims

- Demonstration data are synthetic.
- Priority rules are workflow examples, not financial or legal advice.
- Priority does not estimate likelihood of payment, default, creditworthiness, collectability, or legal status.
- No bank, payment, accounting, email, messaging, customer, or private-data integration exists.
- No automated collections action exists.
- The public build demonstrates deterministic queue logic and inspectability; it does not prove business impact, revenue lift, collection-rate improvement, production-scale readiness, or superiority to other methods.
