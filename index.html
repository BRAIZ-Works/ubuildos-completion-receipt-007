Invoice Follow-Up Queue™

Prioritize manual invoice follow-up with visible aging, priority, reasons, and human override—without touching money, accounts, or customer messaging.

Synthetic demo data only

What this does—and does not do

Does

  • Compute invoice aging from a fixed demo date.
  • Assign transparent follow-up priority.
  • Show the reason for every computed priority.
  • Allow a human override with a required reason.
  • Export the current queue as CSV.

Does not

  • Connect to banks, payment rails, or accounting accounts.
  • Collect money or send customer messages.
  • Predict default, credit risk, collectability, or legal outcomes.
  • Use real customer or private financial data.

Visible queue logic

Demo date: . Missing or invalid required dates fail closed to REVIEW.

  1. CRITICAL: 31+ days overdue.
  2. HIGH: 15–30 days overdue.
  3. MEDIUM: 1–14 days overdue.
  4. WATCH: due within 7 days.
  5. LOW: due later than 7 days.
  6. REVIEW: missing/invalid due date or unresolved input.

Why manual override stays visible

The original computed priority is preserved. An override changes the effective queue position only after a human supplies a reason, so the decision remains inspectable.
